# Ai-Tree
Visualized mobile ai app: a second brain you can see.

Log what you watch, read and think about. ai-tree sorts each entry into a topic and draws your mind as a map: dots are things you saved, lines join related ideas.

## Two tabs
- **Mind**: type a thought, no link needed. Your map, insights (top topics, what to explore next) and recent entries live here. Add `#topic` to file something yourself.
- **Links**: only links you paste or share into the app, with search.

## Post it to TikTok
Tap **Post** for a 1080×1920 image of your map ("What my mind looks like after one month") plus a ready caption. Set your handle under the ⋯ menu.

## Install on your phone
Host the folder on any HTTPS static host (for example GitHub Pages: Settings → Pages → deploy from this branch), open it in Chrome and choose **Add to Home screen**. Once installed, ai-tree shows up in the Android share sheet, so you can share a TikTok or YouTube video straight into it without copying the link.

Everything is stored on your device (localStorage). Use ⋯ → Copy backup to keep a copy.
