
=== large-L deflation: is it the estimator or the sets? ===
A flat control cannot settle this -- a flat control is what hid the
first bug in this statistic. The matched-density Poisson control has
the primes' density profile and independent draws. One draw is not
enough either: at the largest L only ~27 overlapping windows exist, so
the statistic itself is noisy. Hence an ensemble.

imposes near-regular spacing. The unthinned set has the sieve
structure with nothing imposed on its counts.
